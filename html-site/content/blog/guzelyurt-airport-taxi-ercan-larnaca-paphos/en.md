<p>There are three ways into Güzelyurt, and each starts at an airport: Ercan in the north, Larnaca and Paphos in the south. Which one you land at is not only a question of ticket price — it decides how long you spend on the road, whether you cross the Green Line, and what your <strong>Güzelyurt airport taxi</strong> costs. Here are the real figures for all three: distance, drive time, one-way and return fare.</p>

<h2>Ercan Airport — the shortest run</h2>
<p>Ercan is the closest airport to Güzelyurt: about <strong>52 km and 50 minutes</strong>, entirely in the north, with <strong>no border crossing</strong>. The road skirts the top of Nicosia and changes little through the day, which makes the departure time for an early flight easy to work out. Almost every flight connects through Turkey, so the ticket usually means one change — but the drive at the other end is the shortest of the three.</p>
<p>A delayed landing is not a problem on this run: book with your flight number and the car waits for the new arrival time at the same fare.</p>

<h2>Larnaca Airport — where most direct flights land</h2>
<p>Larnaca is in the south and carries most of the direct flights from Europe. The drive to Güzelyurt is roughly <strong>100 km and 105 minutes</strong>, crossing the Green Line at a checkpoint. It is routine: you stay in the car, keep your passport to hand, and there is no charge for passengers. For Güzelyurt we use the <strong>Bostancı (Astromeritis) crossing</strong> rather than routing through Nicosia — it is the faster way west.</p>
<p>Larnaca's advantage is the choice of flights, and often a direct one; the cost is an extra hour on the road and one checkpoint.</p>

<h2>Paphos Airport — the western gateway</h2>
<p>Paphos looks close to Güzelyurt on a map, but the road loops around the west of the island: about <strong>115 km and 110 minutes</strong>, <strong>£120</strong> one way. That is why it is both longer and dearer than Larnaca. Still, if you have found a cheap or well-timed direct flight into Paphos, the run works, and it crosses at Bostancı as well.</p>

<h2>Which airport should you fly into?</h2>
<p>The decision gets clear once you add the transfer to the ticket:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Ercan</strong> — shortest drive, no crossing, lowest transfer fare. If you are happy to connect through Turkey, it is the practical choice for Güzelyurt.</li>
  <li><strong>Larnaca</strong> — easiest place to find a direct flight. You pay about £25 more and roughly an extra hour compared with Ercan.</li>
  <li><strong>Paphos</strong> — worth it only when the flight is markedly cheaper or better timed; it is the longest and most expensive run on the ground.</li>
</ul>
<p>The way to tell whether a flight is really cheaper is to set the ticket difference against the transfer difference: the £65 gap between Paphos and Ercan often swallows it.</p>

<h2>Travelling as a group or with luggage</h2>
<p>The fixed price is per car, so for a family of four the one-way fare divides into a modest cost per head. For groups of up to seven and extra luggage there is a Vito-class vehicle: <strong>Larnaca – Güzelyurt £100</strong> (£180 return) and <strong>Paphos – Güzelyurt £140</strong> (£250 return). <h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Which airport is closest to Güzelyurt?</h3>
<p>Ercan Airport. It is about 52 km and 50 minutes from Güzelyurt, the drive stays in the north, and there is no border crossing. The taxi fare is £55 one way and £100 return.</p>
<h3>How much is a taxi from Ercan Airport to Güzelyurt?</h3>
<p>£55 one way and £100 return. The fare is per car, fixed before you set off, with no meter running, and it is the same in both directions.</p>
<h3>How long does a taxi from Larnaca to Güzelyurt take?</h3>
<p>About 105 minutes over 100 km. The drive crosses the Green Line at a checkpoint; for Güzelyurt we use the faster Bostancı (Astromeritis) crossing rather than Nicosia. The one-way fare is £80.</p>
<h3>What do I have to do at the border crossing?</h3>
<p>In practice, nothing. You stay in the car with your passport to hand, an officer usually stamps a separate slip rather than your passport, and there is no fee for passengers. The crossing adds 20–30 minutes to the drive.</p>
<h3>What happens if my flight is delayed?</h3>
<p>Nothing changes for you. Give your flight number when you book and the arrival is tracked, so the car waits for the new time at the same fare.</p>
<h3>Can I get a taxi for a late-night arrival?</h3>
<p>Yes, but book ahead. Nothing waits on the street in Güzelyurt at night, so airport pickups are arranged in advance around the flight time.</p>
