Most direct flights from Europe land at Larnaca, but Güzelyurt sits at the far end of the plain: about 100 kilometres and one border crossing away. A **Güzelyurt Larnaca Airport taxi** handles both in a single car — terminal to your door, or your door to the terminal, with no change of vehicle. Below is how the run works, how long it takes, when to leave and what to tell us when you book.

## How long does it take?

From central Güzelyurt to Larnaca Airport is about **100 km** and roughly **105 minutes**. That time includes the border crossing. The checkpoint usually adds 20–30 minutes; the rest is motorway and plain road.

Both directions are the same line and work the same way:

- [Güzelyurt – Larnaca Airport](/en/routes/guzelyurt-to-larnaca-airport/) — out to your flight
- [Larnaca Airport – Güzelyurt](/en/routes/larnaca-airport-to-guzelyurt/) — from landing to home

If the pickup is in Lefke, Gemikonağı or one of the surrounding villages, add a few minutes; we work out the exact time from your address when you book.

## The border crossing: Bostancı

The fastest way south from Güzelyurt is not Nicosia but the **Bostancı (Astromeritis)** crossing at the eastern end of the plain. Using the Nicosia crossings means driving east first and then doubling back south-west; Bostancı joins the main southern road directly.

### What happens at the checkpoint?

There are two checks a few hundred metres apart: one leaving the north, one entering the south. You stay in the car for both. You hand over your passport, the officer looks, and the car moves on. Passengers pay nothing; the car's papers and insurance are the driver's job.

### What should you carry?

**Your passport.** At the checkpoint a passport is asked for rather than an ID card, and that applies to everyone in the car. Visa and stamp rules can vary by nationality; the details are in the [North Cyprus passport and border crossing guide](/en/blog/north-cyprus-visa-passport-border-crossing/). For more on the crossing itself, see the [Bostancı crossing guide](/en/blog/guzelyurt-bostanci-crossing-guide/).

## Flying out: when to leave

At Larnaca you need enough time at the airport for check-in and security before your flight; go by what your airline recommends. Add the 105-minute drive and a small margin. In practice most passengers leave Güzelyurt about **four hours before departure**.

Give us your flight time and number and we set the pickup time with you. For early-morning departures, confirm the car the evening before — there are almost no taxis waiting on the street in Güzelyurt at night. The [Güzelyurt night taxi guide](/en/blog/guzelyurt-night-taxi/) covers those hours.

## Landing at Larnaca: how the meet works

Your driver waits in the arrivals hall, opposite the exit doors, holding a sign with your name. They take care of the luggage, walk you to the parked car and confirm your destination before setting off. Every step after landing is in the [Larnaca Airport arrival guide](/en/blog/larnaca-airport-arrival-guide/).

### What if the flight is delayed?

When you give your flight number at booking, the landing is tracked. If the plane is late, the driver waits for the actual landing time, not the scheduled one, and the wait is not added to the fare. Even on a flight that lands after midnight, you will not have to walk out of the terminal looking for a car.

## How is the fare set?

The fare is **per car, not per person**. It is given and fixed before the car moves; no meter runs and there is no haggling on the way. You get the figure when you book or over the phone.

One car is enough for a family of four, and the fare is shared between four. Groups of up to seven or extra luggage go in a Vito-class vehicle. Students arriving at METU Northern Cyprus or European University of Lefke at the start of term will find the details in the [campus transfer guide](/en/blog/metu-ncc-lefke-campus-transfer-guide/).

## Larnaca or Ercan?

The closest airport to Güzelyurt is Ercan: about 50 minutes and no border crossing. Larnaca's advantage is the choice of flights — often direct. The cost is an extra hour on the road and a checkpoint. When deciding, put the ticket difference next to the transfer difference. All three airports are compared in the [Güzelyurt airport taxi guide](/en/blog/guzelyurt-airport-taxi-ercan-larnaca-paphos/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is a taxi from Güzelyurt to Larnaca Airport?

About 105 minutes for 100 km. That includes the border crossing at Bostancı, which usually adds 20–30 minutes.

### Can I cross the border by taxi from Larnaca to Güzelyurt?

Yes. The car crosses the border with you; there is no need to change vehicles. At the checkpoint you stay in the car and show your passport.

### Is an ID card enough for the border crossing?

A passport is asked for at the checkpoint, so keep it with you. Rules can vary by nationality, so check your own conditions before you travel.

### What time should I leave Güzelyurt for my flight?

For most passengers, about four hours before departure. Give us your flight time and we work out the pickup time with you based on your airline's check-in window.

### Will the driver wait if my flight is delayed?

Yes. When you book with your flight number, the landing is tracked; the driver waits for the actual landing time and the wait is not added to the fare.

### How is the Güzelyurt Larnaca Airport taxi fare worked out?

The fare is per car, fixed before the car moves, and no meter runs. You get the figure when you book or over the phone; the same arrangement applies in both directions.

### Can I get a taxi for a late-night arrival or an early-morning departure?

Yes, but it has to be arranged in advance. For early departures, confirm the car the evening before; for night arrivals, the driver waits for you in the arrivals hall.
