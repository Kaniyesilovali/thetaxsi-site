For most of the year Güzelyurt is a quiet town on a citrus plain. Orange Festival week is the exception: stalls, concerts and crowds from all over the island. Getting there is easy; the hard part is getting home when the concert ends near midnight and everyone leaves at once. That is why planning a **Güzelyurt Orange Festival taxi** is less about the trip in and more about the trip back. Below is how long it takes from where, how to set up the return, and what to tell us when you book.

## When is the festival?

The Orange Festival is held in Güzelyurt every year and is the busiest week in town. In recent years it has run in the **last week of June**, for about a week. The exact dates and the concert line-up are announced closer to the festival each year. Plan your trip around that year's programme, especially the day and time of the concert you want to see.

## How long does it take from where?

Most festival visitors come from the other towns in the north. Approximate times:

- [Nicosia – Güzelyurt](/en/routes/nicosia-to-guzelyurt/): 45 km, about 35 minutes
- [Kyrenia – Güzelyurt](/en/routes/kyrenia-to-guzelyurt/): 55 km, about 45 minutes
- [Famagusta – Güzelyurt](/en/routes/famagusta-to-guzelyurt/): 105 km, about 75 minutes
- [Lefke – Güzelyurt](/en/routes/lefke-to-guzelyurt/): 22 km, about 20 minutes

During festival week the way into town slows down and the area around the square is crowded. Allow a few extra minutes on the way in. The lines within the north are covered in [Güzelyurt taxi to Nicosia, Kyrenia and Famagusta](/en/blog/guzelyurt-taxi-nicosia-kyrenia-famagusta/).

### Flying in

If you are flying in for the festival and heading straight to Güzelyurt, the nearest airport is Ercan: about 50 minutes, no border crossing. If you land at Larnaca or Paphos, the road goes through the Bostancı crossing. All three airports are compared in the [Güzelyurt airport taxi guide](/en/blog/guzelyurt-airport-taxi-ercan-larnaca-paphos/).

## The real issue: getting home after the concert

The concerts start in the evening and most finish late at night. A crowd leaves the grounds at the same moment and heads for the same roads. Güzelyurt has few taxis waiting on the street at night anyway; on a festival night, finding a car by phone at the last minute gets harder still.

So arrange the return **before you go**. There are two ways:

- **A timed return pickup.** You are dropped off and collected again at an agreed time and place. If you know when the concert ends, this is the cleanest option.
- **Pickup on request.** You reserve the car for the night in advance and message on WhatsApp when you are ready to leave. This suits you if the time is uncertain, but still confirm the night ahead.

For how night trips work in general, see the [Güzelyurt night taxi guide](/en/blog/guzelyurt-night-taxi/).

### Agree the pickup point up front

When a concert lets out, the road right in front of the grounds fills with cars, and phones struggle in the crowd. Instead of the main entrance, pick an easy-to-describe spot a few minutes' walk away: a junction, a petrol station or a well-known shop. The driver shows you the spot at drop-off, and you meet there on the way back.

## Why not take your own car?

Parking in town is hard on a festival night, and at the end everyone moves at once. More importantly, if you want a drink during the evening, you should not be driving. With a taxi, the group goes together, comes back together, and nobody has to be the driver.

## How is the fare set?

The fare is **per car, not per person**. It is given before you set off and then fixed: no meter, and it does not change with the festival crowds. You get the figure from Denizli Taksi when you book or over the phone. Ask about the trip in and the return together; both are agreed up front. If you come as a group of friends, the fare is split between everyone in the car.

## Four things to tell us when you book

- **Which day, which concert.** The night of the festival week you are coming sets the return time.
- **Your pickup address.** Nicosia, Kyrenia, Famagusta or a village; street and a landmark for the house. For village pickups, see the [Güzelyurt and Lefke villages guide](/en/blog/guzelyurt-lefke-village-taxi/).
- **Your return plan.** A timed pickup, or pickup on request?
- **A number with WhatsApp.** This is how you reach the driver after the concert.

Festival week is the busiest time of year in the area, so the earlier you book, the easier it is. You can [leave the booking in writing in two minutes](/en/book/), or see the whole set-up for the area on the [Güzelyurt Taxi](/en/areas/guzelyurt-taxi/) page. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the Güzelyurt Orange Festival?

It is held every year; in recent years it has run in the last week of June, for about a week. The exact dates and the concert programme are announced closer to the festival.

### How long does a taxi from Nicosia to the Orange Festival take?

About 35 minutes for 45 km. During festival week, allow a few extra minutes getting into town.

### Can I get a taxi home late at night after a concert?

Yes, arranged in advance. Finding a car at the last minute on a festival night is hard; confirm a timed return or a pickup on request before you go.

### Where do I meet the driver after the concert?

At an easy-to-describe spot near the grounds rather than the main entrance: a junction or a well-known shop. You agree the spot together at drop-off.

### How is the Güzelyurt Orange Festival taxi fare calculated?

The fare is per car, fixed before you set off, and there is no meter. You get the figure when you book or over the phone; the trip in and the return are agreed up front.

### How far ahead should I book for the festival?

As early as you can. Festival week is the busiest time in the area, and returns on concert nights fill up first.
