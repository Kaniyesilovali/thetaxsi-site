<p>A <strong>METU Northern Cyprus Campus taxi and transfer</strong> connects the Kalkanlı campus to Guzelyurt town centre, Ercan Airport and the Lefke coast at a fixed price. The campus sits about 10 km from central Guzelyurt, about 20 km from the European University of Lefke and about 60 km from Ercan Airport. This guide gathers every route variation students and visitors ask about, with times and fares side by side.</p>

<h2>Where is METU Northern Cyprus Campus?</h2>
<p>METU Northern Cyprus Campus stands in the village of <strong>Kalkanlı</strong>, part of the Guzelyurt (Morphou) district. It sits on the main road between Guzelyurt town centre and the Gemikonağı–Lefke coast, which means every journey to campus is also a leg of the Guzelyurt–Lefke line. Being at the western end of the island, there are almost no taxis waiting on the street here — journeys are booked in advance.</p>
<p>Two more universities share the same western corridor: the <strong>European University of Lefke (EUL)</strong> and the <strong>University of Health and Social Sciences of Cyprus</strong>. All three campuses use the same transfer line, so the times below follow on from one another.</p>

<h2>METU to Ercan Airport transfer</h2>
<p>METU Northern Cyprus Campus is roughly <strong>60 km and 55–60 minutes</strong> from Ercan Airport. The journey runs along the Guzelyurt–Nicosia main road with no border crossing. Pricing follows the Guzelyurt line: the <a href="/en/routes/ercan-airport-to-guzelyurt/">Ercan Airport to Guzelyurt transfer</a> is <strong>£55</strong> one way and <strong>£100</strong> return. The extra leg out to Kalkanlı is confirmed when you book and fixed before you travel. For the return direction, the <a href="/en/routes/guzelyurt-to-ercan-airport/">Guzelyurt to Ercan Airport transfer</a> page prices the same line.</p>
<p>September and early February are the busiest weeks of the year for campus transfers. Three things make those journeys easy:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Flight tracking</strong> — a delayed landing never costs you your car; the driver waits for the new time</li>
  <li><strong>Luggage capacity</strong> — ask for a 7-seat Vito instead of a saloon for start-of-term bags</li>
  <li><strong>Splitting the fare</strong> — friends on the same flight share one car and one price</li>
</ul>
<p>For what happens between landing and the exit door, see our <a href="/en/blog/ercan-airport-arrival-guide/">Ercan Airport arrival guide</a>.</p>

<h2>METU to Lefke and its variations</h2>
<p>METU's Kalkanlı campus is about <strong>20 km and 20–25 minutes</strong> from Lefke centre. This is the busiest campus-to-campus line in the region: students from both universities, trips to the coast and accommodation in Lefke all share the same road. The variations people ask for:</p>
<ul>
  <li>METU campus ↔ Lefke town centre</li>
  <li>METU campus ↔ Gemikonağı beach and harbour</li>
  <li>METU campus ↔ European University of Lefke grounds</li>
  <li>METU campus ↔ student halls and apartments in Lefke</li>
</ul>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>How far is METU Northern Cyprus Campus from Ercan Airport?</h3>
<p>About 60 km. The journey takes 55–60 minutes along the Guzelyurt–Nicosia main road and involves no border crossing. The fare is built on the Ercan–Guzelyurt line (£55 one way) plus the Kalkanlı leg, and is fixed when you book.</p>
<h3>How long does a taxi take from METU campus to Lefke?</h3>
<p>About 20–25 minutes over roughly 20 km. Because Kalkanlı sits on the road between Guzelyurt and Gemikonağı, Lefke centre, the European University of Lefke and the Gemikonağı seafront all line up along the same route.</p>
<h3>How do I get to the European University of Lefke from the airport?</h3>
<p>From Ercan Airport it is about 70 minutes and £80 (£145 return). From Larnaca it is 120 minutes and £120, from Paphos 95 minutes and £110. The Larnaca and Paphos routes cross the border, so keep your passport with you.</p>
<h3>How much is a taxi from Guzelyurt centre to METU campus?</h3>
<p>It runs at a fixed local fare as a short district journey — about 10 km and 12–15 minutes. The price is agreed before you set off and no meter is used.</p>
<h3>I have a lot of luggage at the start of term — which vehicle?</h3>
<p>A saloon car is comfortable with up to two large suitcases; beyond that, ask for a 7-seat Vito. Vito fares are listed separately on the Larnaca and Paphos routes — £100 from Larnaca and £140 from Paphos to Guzelyurt.</p>
<h3>Can I get back to campus late at night?</h3>
<p>Yes. There are almost no taxis waiting on the street in western Cyprus after dark, which is exactly why late campus returns are booked ahead. Once the time and pick-up point are set, the car is there at the agreed hour.</p>
<h3>Can we split the fare with friends?</h3>
<p>Yes. The fixed price is per vehicle, not per person. Four people arriving on the same flight can share a saloon, and six or seven can share a Vito, which brings the cost per person down substantially.</p>
