<p>Güzelyurt (Morphou) goes quiet early in the evening. By day you will see a few cars in the centre; after dark there is almost no taxi waiting on the street or cruising for fares. That doesn't mean there is no car at night — it means the method is different. A <strong>Güzelyurt night taxi</strong> works by giving the time in advance, not by walking out to find one. Below are the most common night trips and how the car is secured for each.</p>

<h2>Why is there no taxi on the street at night?</h2>
<p>Güzelyurt is a town on a plain; its people are spread across the surrounding villages and campuses rather than packed into the centre. There simply isn't enough density at night for a car to drive around waiting for fares. So night trips are planned around times agreed in advance: who, from where, at what time. Once the time is set, the car is set aside for it and is at the door.</p>
<p>Phone and WhatsApp are answered day and night, so a message sent at midnight still gets a reply — but if you need a car for that same hour, the earlier you let us know, the surer the car.</p>

<h2>Pre-dawn airport departures</h2>
<p>This is the trip a night taxi is used for most. For early flights the departure time falls after midnight, and at that hour there is only a car if it was arranged the evening before.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Ercan Airport:</strong> about 50 minutes, all in the north. With no traffic at night, the time is predictable.</li>
  <li><strong>Larnaca and Paphos:</strong> longer roads and a crossing point. The crossing works at night too, but we leave a margin for it when setting the departure time.</li>
</ul>
<p>Give us your flight time and flight number and we work out when you need to leave. Each line is covered in detail in the <a href="/en/blog/guzelyurt-airport-taxi-ercan-larnaca-paphos/">Güzelyurt airport taxi guide</a>.</p>

<h2>Flights landing at night</h2>
<p>If you are arriving on a flight that lands at Ercan or Larnaca around midnight, the car waits for you at the airport. When you give the flight number at booking, the landing is tracked: if the flight is delayed, the driver waits for the actual landing time rather than the scheduled one, and the wait isn't added to the fare. The worst case for a night arrival is walking out of the terminal and looking for a car to Güzelyurt; a pickup arranged in advance takes that off the table.</p>

<h2>Late returns from Kyrenia and Nicosia</h2>
<p>Dinner, a wedding, a concert — most people who go into town from Güzelyurt come back at night. The problem isn't getting there, it is getting back: finding a car in Kyrenia or Nicosia at midnight that will drive to Güzelyurt is hard and means haggling.</p>
<p>The cleanest way is to book the return together with the outbound. Give an estimated return time and the car is set aside for it; if you finish early or late, just call. Times and patterns for these lines are in the <a href="/en/blog/guzelyurt-taxi-nicosia-kyrenia-famagusta/">Güzelyurt taxi to Nicosia, Kyrenia and Famagusta</a> guide.</p>

<h2>Students: campus and dorm returns</h2>
<p>Classes, labs and events at METU Northern Cyprus Campus and European University of Lefke can finish late. Getting back from campus to a dorm or to Güzelyurt centre late at night is a normal part of night trips. If you are going back as a group, one car may be enough — the fare is per car, not per person. Details are in the <a href="/en/blog/metu-ncc-lefke-campus-transfer-guide/">METU and Lefke student taxi guide</a>.</p>

<h2>Four rules for a night taxi</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Give the time in advance.</strong> The evening before for pre-dawn departures, together with the outbound for late returns.</li>
  <li><strong>Be precise about the pickup point.</strong> Signs are hard to read on village roads at night; the village name, the street and something that marks the house — the colour of the door, the shop next to it — make the driver's job easy. For village pickups, see the <a href="/en/blog/guzelyurt-lefke-village-taxi/">villages guide</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Give a number with WhatsApp.</strong> The driver messages at pickup, so you see the car has arrived before stepping out at night.</li>
  <li><strong>Agree the fare up front.</strong> At night too, the fare is quoted and fixed before you set off; there is no meter, and you pay the driver in cash on arrival.</li>
</ul>
<p>The whole booking process, step by step, is in <a href="/en/blog/guzelyurt-taxi-how-to-book/">how to book a Güzelyurt taxi</a>.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>Is there a night taxi in Güzelyurt?</h3>
<p>Yes, by arrangement. There is almost no car waiting on the street at night; give the time in advance and the car is at the door at the agreed hour. Phone and WhatsApp are answered day and night.</p>
<h3>Can I get a taxi to Ercan Airport before dawn?</h3>
<p>Yes. Give your flight time the evening before; we work out the departure time together and the car is at your door at that hour. Ercan is about 50 minutes from Güzelyurt, with no crossing.</p>
<h3>Will the car wait for my flight landing at midnight?</h3>
<p>Yes. When you give the flight number, the landing is tracked; if the flight is delayed, the driver waits for the actual landing time and the wait isn't added to the fare.</p>
<h3>How do I get back from Kyrenia to Güzelyurt at night?</h3>
<p>Book the return together with the outbound. Give an estimated return time and the car is set aside for it; if you finish early or late, just call.</p>
<h3>How is the fare for a night trip set?</h3>
<p>The same as by day: the fare is quoted per trip by phone or at booking, fixed before you set off and paid to the driver in cash on arrival. It is per car, not per person.</p>
<h3>Can I call a night taxi from a village?</h3>
<p>Yes. The villages around Güzelyurt and Lefke are on the same line. Give the village name, the street and a description of the house; at night that description is what lets the car find you first time.</p>
