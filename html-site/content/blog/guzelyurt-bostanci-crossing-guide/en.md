<p>The Güzelyurt plain has its own door to the south. Getting to Larnaca or Paphos does not mean driving east to Nicosia and doubling back: the <strong>Bostancı (Astromeritis)</strong> crossing at the eastern edge of the plain is the quick way out of this side of the island. Here is how a <strong>Güzelyurt taxi</strong> handles a run to the south — what happens at the gate, what you need with you, and which crossing is used for which journey.</p>

<h2>Why Bostancı rather than the Nicosia gates</h2>
<p>On a map the border looks like a single line, but the gates along it are not interchangeable. The Nicosia crossings sit inside city traffic, and reaching them from Güzelyurt means driving forty-five kilometres east only to turn back southwest. Bostancı sits at the eastern edge of the plain and feeds straight onto the main road south.</p>
<p>So every southern run from the western lines goes through here — Larnaca, Paphos, Limassol. For the airport lines in full, see the <a href="/en/blog/guzelyurt-airport-taxi-ercan-larnaca-paphos/">Güzelyurt airport taxi guide</a>.</p>

<h2>What happens at the gate</h2>
<p>The crossing is shorter than most people expect. There are two checks a few hundred metres apart — one leaving the north, one entering the south — and you stay in the car for both. You hand over your passport, the officer looks, you drive on.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Passengers are not charged to cross.</strong> The vehicle's paperwork and insurance are the driver's business, not yours.</li>
  <li><strong>Keep your passport with you.</strong> It is a passport check rather than an ID check, and it applies to everyone in the car.</li>
  <li><strong>Queues, when they happen, are usually short</strong> — though for an early flight, booking the car the day before beats guessing at the morning traffic.</li>
</ul>
<p>For visas, stamps and passport detail, see <a href="/en/blog/north-cyprus-visa-passport-border-crossing/">North Cyprus passports and border crossings</a>. Rules can change, so check the conditions for your own nationality before you travel.</p>

<h2>Southern runs through Bostancı</h2>
<h3>Larnaca Airport</h3>
<p>About <strong>100 km and 105 minutes</strong> from Güzelyurt. It is the busier of the two southern airports and takes most of the direct flights from Europe. The line: <a href="/en/routes/guzelyurt-to-larnaca-airport/">Guzelyurt to Larnaca Airport</a>, and the return direction, <a href="/en/routes/larnaca-airport-to-guzelyurt/">Larnaca Airport to Guzelyurt</a>.</p>
<h3>Paphos Airport</h3>
<p>About <strong>115 km and 110 minutes</strong> from Güzelyurt — the road loops around the west coast, so it is longer than it looks on a map. From Lefke the picture changes: at <strong>98 km and 95 minutes</strong>, Paphos is the shortest southern run there is. The lines: <a href="/en/routes/guzelyurt-to-paphos-airport/">Guzelyurt to Paphos Airport</a> and <a href="/en/routes/lefke-to-paphos-airport/">Lefke to Paphos Airport</a>.</p>
<h3>Limassol and the southern towns</h3>
<p>Limassol is about <strong>105 km and 100 minutes</strong>. This is the line for the port, the shops, or a day out in the south; the drive again goes through Bostancı and the fare is agreed before you set off. The line: <a href="/en/routes/guzelyurt-to-limassol/">Guzelyurt to Limassol</a>.</p>

<h2>Yeşilırmak (Limnitis): the west's second gate</h2>
<p>The region has one more door to the south. <strong>Yeşilırmak (Limnitis)</strong> sits at the western tip and is noticeably closer to Lefke and the coastal villages than Bostancı. Two limits to plan around: it opens in daylight hours only, and it takes cars rather than foot traffic.</p>
<p>That is why pre-dawn departures and late-night landings usually route back through Bostancı. Which gate gets used is something the driver settles when the job is booked — you give the time, the line picks the crossing. <h2>Common questions</h2>
<h3>Which crossing do you use from Güzelyurt to the south?</h3>
<p>Bostancı (Astromeritis). It sits at the eastern edge of the plain and feeds straight onto the main road south, so the Nicosia gates would mean an unnecessary trip east and back again.</p>
<h3>Can a taxi take me through the Bostancı crossing?</h3>
<p>Yes. The car crosses with you and there is no changing vehicles on the other side. Larnaca, Paphos and Limassol runs are all done door to door in one car.</p>
<h3>Do passengers pay anything at the border?</h3>
<p>No. You stay in the car, show your passport and carry on. The vehicle's paperwork and insurance are the driver's responsibility.</p>
<h3>How long is Güzelyurt to Larnaca Airport?</h3>
<p>About 100 km and 105 minutes via Bostancı. Book with your flight number and the landing time is tracked.</p>
<h3>What is the nearest crossing to Lefke?</h3>
<p>Yeşilırmak (Limnitis), noticeably closer than Bostancı — but it opens in daylight hours only, so agree the timing in advance if you are coming back late.</p>
<h3>Do I need my passport to cross south?</h3>
<p>You will be asked for it at the check, so keep it with you. Rules can change, so confirm the conditions for your own nationality before you travel.</p>
