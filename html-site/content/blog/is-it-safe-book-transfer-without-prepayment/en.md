---
title: "Is It Safe to Book a Transfer Without Paying Upfront?"
description: "How to book a North Cyprus airport transfer with confidence — no prepayment, driver details in advance, and simple checks that a company is genuine."
---
<p>Yes — booking a transfer without paying upfront is safe when the company gives you a written confirmation, sends the driver's name, phone and vehicle before you travel, and is a licensed operator. In fact, paying the driver on arrival protects you: you only pay once the car is actually there. Here is how to book with confidence.</p>

<h2>Why is no prepayment safer for you?</h2>
<p>When you pay on arrival, the money changes hands only after the driver has met you and the car is in front of you. There is no deposit to chase and nothing lost if plans change. It shifts the risk away from you — the company earns the fare by showing up, which is exactly the incentive you want.</p>

<h2>How do I know the driver will actually be there?</h2>
<p>A genuine operator removes the guesswork before you land. You should receive, in advance:</p>
<ol>
  <li>A written booking confirmation with your route, time and fixed fare.</li>
  <li>The driver's name and phone number, so you can reach them directly.</li>
  <li>The vehicle details and a meeting point — usually a name sign inside arrivals.</li>
</ol>
<p>With those in hand, you are not hoping a car appears; you know who is meeting you and can message them the moment you land.</p>

<h2>How can I check a transfer company is genuine?</h2>
<p>A few quick checks tell you most of what you need. Look for a licence — in North Cyprus, a KITSAB registration number signals a registered operator. Check that prices are published per route rather than hidden behind "contact us." And confirm there is a real contact channel, such as a working WhatsApp number that answers before you book.</p>

<h2>How do I pay on arrival?</h2>
<p>You pay the driver in cash once you have been met and the car is in front of you — there is no prepayment and no deposit. Many drivers accept pounds, euros or Turkish lira, so agree the currency when you book. Because nothing leaves your account until the car is there, paying on arrival keeps the risk on the company, not on you.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Do I have to create an account to book?</h3>
<p>No. A booking should take a couple of minutes over a form or WhatsApp, with no account and no upfront payment required.</p>
<h3>What if I need to cancel?</h3>
<p>Because there is no deposit, cancelling before the trip costs you nothing. Let the company know as early as you can so the driver can be reassigned.</p>
<h3>How do I recognise my driver at the airport?</h3>
<p>Your driver waits in the arrivals hall with a sign showing your name, and you already have their name, phone and vehicle from your confirmation.</p>
